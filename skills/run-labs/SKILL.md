# Skill: run-labs

Interactive lab sessions. These are vendor sandboxes (Azure, NVIDIA and similar) provisioned for the
learner and torn down after. They are hands-on: the learner drives, the coach navigates and watches.

## Before

- Check the lab URL and credentials are current. **Provisioning takes about four minutes** - start it,
  then do something else rather than staring at it.
- **"Ready" in the lab launcher does not mean ready.** The VM is up before the services inside it are.
  Wait for the actual service to respond, then start.
- New credentials are generated **every time** a lab is provisioned. Never reuse from a previous
  session and never write them anywhere permanent.

## During

- The learner types, the coach points. Doing it for them defeats the exercise.
- Keep a running note of what was actually clicked and what actually happened - lab portals differ
  from their documentation, and the difference is exactly what the write-up needs.
- If a step fails, record the error verbatim before trying the fix. The error text is evidence.

## After

- **Hold the submission.** A lab is finished when the learner says so, not when the checklist turns
  green. The evidence pack (screenshots, notes on what happened, what went wrong) goes to the learner
  for review before anything is uploaded.
- Lab credentials and scratch VMs are never committed anywhere.
